About Ram Janki Shiksha Niketan High School

Vill + P.O–Mahadeo Chaigri, P.S–Bharno, Dist–Gumla, Jharkhand — 835203

Vision

To provide quality education rooted in local culture, empowering children of Gumla district to become responsible, creative, and skilled citizens.

Mission

  • Deliver a strong academic foundation with emphasis on moral values.
  • Create safe, inclusive learning spaces for all students.
  • Foster arts, sports and vocational skills alongside academics.

Campus & Facilities

Classrooms & Labs

Well-ventilated classrooms, basic science lab, and a small library with learning materials.

Playground & Sports

Open ground for sports and physical activities.

Sanitation & Safety

Separate restrooms for boys/girls and first-aid facilities.

Community Programs

Events, cultural programs, and local skill-building workshops.

How to Reach

Nearest town: Gumla. For official visits, contact Trust at trust.rjsn.gumla@gmail.com or call the school manager.
